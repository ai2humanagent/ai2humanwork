# Ethics and Data Governance Checklist

## Before capture

- Obtain explicit research-use consent.
- State retention duration, access controls, withdrawal process, and public-release boundary.
- Collect only evidence required by the Proof Policy.
- Exclude legal, medical, employment, credit, biometric, intimate, or covert-surveillance decisions.
- Do not induce participants to violate platform rules or disclose third-party private data.

## Storage

- Raw evidence stays in access-controlled product storage.
- Research records contain commitments, observations, versions, and privacy-scoped references.
- Separate participant reference from analysis identifiers.
- Log access and deletion.

## Independent raters

- Compensate raters regardless of agreement with authors.
- Blind product outcome, settlement result, automated final verdict, and other ratings.
- Permit “insufficient to decide.”
- Preserve disagreement before adjudication.

## Publication

- Publish only consented/redacted artifacts.
- Report exclusions, missingness, failures, conflicts, and negative results.
- Do not release attack details that materially enable abuse of live systems without mitigation.
- Document whether institutional ethics review, exemption, or equivalent review was obtained.
