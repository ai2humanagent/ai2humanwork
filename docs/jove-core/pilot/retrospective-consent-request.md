# Retrospective Research Consent Request

We previously processed a digital task through AI2Human. We would like permission to include a privacy-minimized research record from that task in a study of evidence verification.

The research record would include the task request, compiled evidence policy, cryptographic evidence commitment, verifier observations, decision state, timing/cost metadata, and a receipt. It would exclude raw artifact URLs, usernames, wallet addresses, filenames, private messages, and unrelated account data.

Participation is optional. Declining has no effect on the original task or payment. If you consent, the consent record will specify retention duration, withdrawal procedure, and whether any redacted artifact may be published. Without explicit consent, the task will not enter the research dataset.

Consent response required: `I consent` or `I do not consent`, plus the approved retention period.
