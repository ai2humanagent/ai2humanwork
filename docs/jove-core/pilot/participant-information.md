# Participant Information — JOVE-Core Live Pilot

You are invited to complete a low-risk digital task and submit evidence through AI2Human. The study evaluates whether an automated system can describe evidence requirements and make cautious, auditable decisions.

Participation is voluntary. Do not submit passwords, private messages, financial balances, government identifiers, biometric information, private contact information, or unrelated third-party data. Use participant-owned, research, or test accounts only. Tasks must be harmless and reversible where possible.

The research record stores task text, policy rules, evidence hashes and counts, verifier observations, timing/cost metadata, and a decision receipt. Raw evidence remains access-controlled and is not automatically included in a public dataset. The consent record states the retention period and withdrawal process.

You may stop before submission. A task outcome, including rejection or request for clearer evidence, does not affect compensation promised for participating in the research procedure unless the consent document explicitly states otherwise.
