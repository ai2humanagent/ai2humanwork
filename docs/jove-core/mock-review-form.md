# Independent Mock Review Form

## Scores

- Originality: 1–5
- Technical quality: 1–5
- Empirical rigor: 1–5
- Clarity: 1–5
- Reproducibility: 1–5
- Ethics/claim discipline: 1–5
- Overall: Reject / Weak Reject / Weak Accept / Accept

## Mandatory questions

1. Is the problem distinct from LLM-as-a-Judge and ordinary pipeline engineering?
2. Does the formalization constrain the method or merely rename implementation fields?
3. Is J1-IM a sufficiently strong information-matched primary baseline (and J1+abstain a sufficiently strong policy-aware reference)?
4. Can matched mutations reveal synthetic artifacts?
5. Are grouped splits and sealed labels credible?
6. Does selective escalation improve risk after accounting for coverage and cost?
7. Does the human study test complementarity rather than free human correction?
8. Which claim is least supported?
9. What single missing experiment would cause rejection?
10. What result would falsify the paper's central thesis?
