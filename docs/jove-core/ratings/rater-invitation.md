# Independent Rater Invitation

We are recruiting independent raters for a developmental study of digital evidence verification. Raters receive blinded task requests, proof policies, and privacy-scoped evidence packets. They do not see the automated final verdict, payment result, other ratings, or author expectations.

Raters classify whether evidence supports the requested outcome, identify evidence defects, mark unnecessary private disclosure, and provide a reason and confidence score. Compensation is based on completed valid ratings, not agreement with the system or authors.

The pilot requires two independent ratings per case. The confirmatory study requires at least three. All disagreements are retained and reported before adjudication.
