# Human Review and Auditability Study

## Design

Randomized within-subject or balanced mixed design comparing:

- Control: task request plus privacy-scoped evidence.
- Treatment: Proof Policy, evidence, deterministic/forensic observations, and structured model judgments without the final system verdict.

## Primary human outcome

False-accept rate. Secondary outcomes are overall accuracy, decision time, confidence, agreement, workload, and appropriate reliance.

## Required preparation

- Complete a pilot-derived variance estimate.
- Run power analysis before recruitment.
- Freeze exclusions and compensation.
- Obtain required ethics review or documented exemption.
- Randomize condition and case order.
- Prevent access to product status and settlement outcome.

## Auditability task

Independent auditors receive either a structured receipt or an unstructured application log and reconstruct: governing rule, decisive evidence, model degradation, escalation reason, and settlement eligibility.

## Stop rule

Do not run the human study until pilot packets are accessible, privacy-audited, and the rating rubric reaches acceptable calibration agreement.
